Details people ask about

Are the characters self-aware or storing my messages?

No. Every reply is generated fiction from language models. On the free plan, server copies of your messages are deleted within seven days — the copy on your device is the one that lasts. Astra+ stores them so sync works between your devices.

What counts against the daily message limit?

One sent message equals one credit, regardless of reply length. The counter resets at midnight UTC, and unused credits do not roll over.

Can I import or edit characters?

You can create bots with a name, greeting, genres, and up to 500 characters of personality instructions, then choose public or private. Editing and deleting stay available to the creator permanently.

How does billing work if I subscribe here?

Checkout runs on Stripe and attaches the subscription to your Astra account. The app picks it up on the next message — no reinstall, no re-login. Cancel from the billing portal whenever; access runs until the period ends.

Where did the character library come from?

A mix of studio originals and community favourites published with attribution to their original creators on Character Tavern.
